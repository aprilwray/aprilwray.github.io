Digital marketing & AI coaching

What do you need a hand with?

Websites, SEO, socials, ads and AI for business. Maybe it’s one thing. Maybe you need a new website, and while we’re there, can we please sort the copy and work out what to post? Either is fine.

They connect on purpose. I use search, and how a site is actually built, to shape the design. Then the marketing and the ads say the same thing. If the site, the ads and the captions met at a party, they should recognise each other.

WebsitesWordPress, Divi and WooCommerce sites, and marketing for brands already on Shopify. SEOA site structure that makes sense, page titles that say something and copy that helps people find you. SocialsContent plans, captions, reels and carousels, including what to post next Tuesday. AdsMeta and Google ads, aimed at one clear next step. AI for businessAI coaching for the jobs that eat your week, and a handover so your team can run them. CopywritingWebsites, emails, product copy and guides, written in words you’d actually say. BrandingVoice, a logo and the look, so the site and the socials feel like the same business. EmailCampaigns, class notes and product emails people have a reason to open. Creative productionProduct photos, video, campaign artwork and the occasional cookbook.

Websites

I work with WordPress, Divi, WooCommerce and Wix. Whether it’s a service site or an online shop, I think through what goes on each page and what people need to find once they’re there. If you already sell on Shopify, I can help with the marketing around your store.

See it in the work: Intuitive Healing with Jo, Nova Holistic Spa, Gold & Sage, Holy Threads, Monument Homes, Tickets in a Hat, Capstone Industries and Sunflower Community Supports.

SEO

I start with what people are likely to search for, then use that to plan the site structure, page titles, descriptions and copy. If your customers call it a freight hoist, the page can probably call it a freight hoist too.

See it in the work: Capstone Industries, Nova Holistic Spa and Sunflower Community Supports.

Socials

I plan content and make the actual posts: captions, carousels, reels and whatever else fits the channel. I can take care of the week-to-week side too, so you’ve got something ready before you need it.

See it in the work: Jo, Nova, Gold & Sage, Tea by Birdy, Kindred Souls, Holy Threads, Tickets in a Hat, Capstone and Sunflower.

Ads

I work with Meta and Google ads. I like starting with one question: what do you want someone to do after they see this?

See it in the work: Tea by Birdy and Sunflower Community Supports.

AI for business

This is the coaching. I help small businesses put simple AI to work on the jobs that eat the week, then show the owner and the team how to run it themselves. That might be a content and marketing workflow, the admin that piles up, a set of prompts and a short how-to, or a session in Claude, ChatGPT or a no-code tool. You should leave able to use it, not with another login you never open.

No client stories on this one yet. If you want it, it starts as a conversation.

Copywriting

I write website pages, emails, guides, blog posts and product copy. I’ll ask how you explain what you do to a real person. There’s usually a better homepage in that conversation than in the words “tailored solutions”.

See it in the work: Intuitive Healing with Jo, Tea by Birdy and Kindred Souls Collective.

Branding

I work on the voice, the look and the basics that hold them together. That can be how you talk, a logo and a simple visual identity, colour and type, and a short set of guidelines. The aim is that the website, the socials and the creative sound and look like the same business.

See it in the work: the Celtic Salt label, calendars and tarot imagery for Intuitive Healing with Jo. Campaign artwork for Gold & Sage and Holy Threads is their brand imagery, shown with the projects. I haven’t claimed a logo for a client unless the page says so.

Email

I write emails for campaigns, classes and product ranges. Same question as the ads: what do you want someone to do after they read this?

See it in the work: Kindred Souls Collective, and the low-carb guide written for the email list at Intuitive Healing with Jo.

Creative production

I shoot product photos and video and work on the bits you might need around a launch or a product range: labels, guides, calendars, cookbooks, tarot imagery and campaign artwork. Yes, a cookbook and tarot images have both made it onto the list.

See it in the work: Intuitive Healing with Jo. For examples of campaign and brand imagery alongside my work with Tomedia, see Gold & Sage and Holy Threads.
