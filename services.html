Services

The work, in one practice.

Websites, search, socials, copy, ads, email and creative production. Each case study says what was actually delivered. Nothing below is a result figure — those stay off the site until there is a source to publish.

Websites

WordPress and Divi, WooCommerce stores, and Wix. Shopify appears when the brief is marketing around a store that already exists — Tea by Birdy is that case, and the theme is not claimed as April’s build.

In the work: Intuitive Healing with Jo, Nova Holistic Spa, Gold & Sage, Holy Threads, Monument Homes, Tickets in a Hat, Capstone Industries, Sunflower Community Supports.

SEO

Information architecture, page titles, meta descriptions and a keyword-led structure. A figure is quoted only when the case study has a source for it.

In the work: Capstone Industries (SEO specialist on the contract), Nova Holistic Spa (website sessions up 67% in July 2024), Sunflower Community Supports (rebuild in progress).

Social media

Channel care, captions, carousels, reels and the planning that keeps a month coherent. Kindred Souls is social and copy only — the studio website is someone else’s credit.

In the work: Intuitive Healing with Jo, Nova, Gold & Sage, Tea by Birdy, Kindred Souls Collective, Holy Threads, Tickets in a Hat, plus both employment roles.

Copywriting

Website and email copy, usage guides, a journal of useful posts, and the lines on a product or a class carousel. Posts are named as hers only once she has marked them.

In the work: Intuitive Healing with Jo, Tea by Birdy journal, Kindred Souls copy audit and carousels.

Ads and email

Meta and Google advertising, and email that belongs to a campaign or a class calendar. Spend, reach and revenue are not published here.

In the work: Tea by Birdy (ads, and Meta Business Manager access until 1 February 2025), Kindred Souls (May email drafts), Sunflower Community Supports (Google Ads and Facebook Ads, no figures).

Creative production

Product photography, videography and editing, plus the printed and digital pieces a range needs: guides, calendars, cookbooks, labels, tarot images and campaign artwork. Photography on a Tomedia store is shown as the brand’s visual, not as a personal photo credit, unless April adds one.

The fullest set is Intuitive Healing with Jo. Campaign and product images also appear on Gold & Sage and Holy Threads.
