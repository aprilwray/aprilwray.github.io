The challenge

A sound-healing studio needed social and email content that felt like the rooms — calm, clear, and easy to book — without leaning on a website April did not build.

What April did

In early 2025 she produced class and education carousels, a month of content strategy under the name Rest & Restoration, a copy audit of the existing site, May email drafts, and studio video.
