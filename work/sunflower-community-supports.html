The website rebuild

I’m rebuilding the site around the way people look for support: what’s available, which hub is closest and how to make a referral or get in touch. That includes planning the page structure and researching the terms people search for. The relaunch is still underway.

What people can find on the public site

The site covers hubs, day programs, allied health, community access, in-home support, accommodation and respite, and support coordination. It also gives people a way to ask a question or make a referral. The public site uses WordPress and Divi.
