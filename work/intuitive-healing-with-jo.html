Intuitive Healing with Jo

A holistic practice and product shop in Airport West, Victoria, looked after as one brief: the website, the socials, and the creative around the range.

Services

The challenge

A long-running practice needed one person to keep the website and the socials moving, and to make the creative that sits around a product range — photography, video, planners, labels and guides — so it did not feel piecemeal.

What April did

She ran the live WordPress shop and the social channels. In November 2023 she put the Kefir product live. Through 2025 and 2026 the work included Facebook page access and Google Business Profile reporting.

She photographed the product range, including Blueberry Probiotic, Liver Tonic, Magnesium Roll-On, Oxygen Water, Turmeric Probiotic, vitamin sprays and Wild Yam Cream. She shot and edited video: essentials and supplements reels, and Journaling with Jo edits and captions still underway in August 2026.

Collateral from the same brief includes the 2024 planner, an informational booklet of usage guides and recipes, a low-carb eating guide written for the email list, a Celtic Salt label draft from November 2023, plus calendars and tarot images.
