Intuitive Healing with Jo

Jo Anastasio runs a holistic practice and product shop in Airport West. I worked across the WordPress shop, socials and the creative around her product range. It’s the sort of brief where updating a product page can turn into shooting a reel and drafting a label in the same week.

What Jo needed

Jo had a range of products to explain and promote. She needed someone who could keep the shop and social channels moving and make the pieces that went with them, from product photos to printed guides.

What I worked on

I looked after the WordPress shop and social channels, put the Kefir product live in November 2023 and worked with Facebook and Google Business Profile reporting through 2025 and 2026.

I photographed products including Blueberry Probiotic, Liver Tonic, Magnesium Roll-On, Oxygen Water, Turmeric Probiotic, vitamin sprays and Wild Yam Cream. I shot and edited essentials and supplements reels, and worked on edits and captions for Journaling with Jo.

On the writing and design side, I worked on a 2024 planner, a booklet with usage guides and recipes, a low-carb eating guide for the email list, a draft Celtic Salt label, calendars and tarot imagery.
