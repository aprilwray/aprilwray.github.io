The challenge

A new tea label needed an online store and marketing with the same unhurried feel as the tins — not a generic shop theme with the logo dropped in.

What April did

Delivered through Tomedia, she delivered the website and a marketing and social suite. The live store still carries the Tomedia credit and a custom theme.
