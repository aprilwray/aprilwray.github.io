Nova is a Hobart spa with a therapeutic bathhouse, massage, skin treatments, counselling and mild hyperbaric oxygen therapy. I worked on its Wix site, marketing and socials through Tomedia.

What Nova needed

The site needed to give people a sense of what it’s like to be there and answer the practical questions: what can I book, what’s in a package and how do I buy a gift voucher?

What I worked on

As part of the Tomedia project, I worked on the Wix site across services, packages, the spa’s story, gift vouchers and online bookings. I also worked on the marketing and socials around the site.
