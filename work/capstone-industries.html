The website

In this role April designed and built the company website, with an SEO-led structure and attention to how the pages are used. Treat the live site as her build, with one clear limit: it has moved on since she left. A Portal item in the main navigation should not be read as part of the 2023–2024 work. In 2025 she was on the client side of a later member-portal commission with Tomedia.

Observed on 25 November 2026: WordPress with the Divi theme. WooCommerce is in the stack, but the public site behaves as a product-information and lead-generation site, not an open shop. SEO is handled with Rank Math. The page language is en-AU. There is no “site by” credit in the footer.

The products hub runs deep: dock levellers and related dock gear, scissor lift tables, goods and freight hoists, spare parts and accessories. The homepage opens with a full-width photograph and three promises — 24-hour emergency service, support from service through to installation, and Australian-made innovation — plus a get-in-touch button, a nationwide-reach block, service tiles, and quote and phone calls to action. Victoria and NSW addresses sit on the page. Social links in the header point to the company’s Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube and Instagram.

Search, as observed

These are tags on the live homepage on 25 November 2026, not a traffic report, and not a frozen copy of the original launch. The title was “Dock Levellers, Scissor Lifts & Hoists | Capstone Industries”. The meta description described premium materials-handling equipment designed and manufactured in Australia, including dock levellers, scissor lifts and freight hoists. The page also had a canonical URL, Open Graph and Twitter Card tags, and JSON-LD for the organisation and the Campbellfield address. Product URLs follow the terms the range is known for.

She also ran a full site audit of the domain on 17 October 2024. That audit is process — she was measuring the site — and it is not published here as a result.

Around the website

The role also covered brochure and spec-sheet design, and social media: a content calendar and captions, with an early plan of about two to three posts a month. She redesigned brochures, spec sheets, manuals and sales PDFs to sit with the new site, and wrote case studies and testimonials for the business. Those pieces are not shown here yet.
