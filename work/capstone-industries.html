Capstone Industries

Capstone makes materials-handling equipment: dock levellers, scissor lifts, hoists and more. I worked part-time across web design, SEO and social. If you’ve ever wondered how much there is to say about a scissor lift, it turns out there’s plenty.

The website I built

I designed and built the website and organised the pages around the products and services people were likely to look for. The site has evolved since then. The member portal was a separate 2025 Tomedia project, which I worked on from the client side.

What’s on the site today

Customers can look up equipment, explore services and request a quote. The product pages cover dock gear, scissor lift tables, goods and freight hoists, spare parts and accessories.

Search and site structure

I ran a full site audit in October 2024. The November 2026 version still uses equipment terms such as dock levellers, scissor lifts and hoists in the page titles and product URLs. The site uses WordPress and Divi, with Rank Math for SEO, and is set up for enquiries.

Away from the website

I redesigned brochures, spec sheets, manuals and sales PDFs to sit alongside the new site. I also put together a social calendar, wrote captions and worked on case studies and testimonial copy for the business.
