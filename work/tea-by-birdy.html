The challenge

A family tea brand already heading for a shopfront needed ongoing marketing — socials, ads, and a journal of useful tea writing — so the brand had a voice beyond the product grid.

What April did

Delivered through Tomedia, she looked after marketing, socials, ads and blog content, and held Meta Business Manager access until it was removed on 1 February 2025.

The public writing sample is the Tea Journal (opens in a new tab). Individual posts are not listed here until April marks which ones are hers. There is also a YouTube channel, youtube.com/@teabybirdy (opens in a new tab).
