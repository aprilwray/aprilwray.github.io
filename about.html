About

I’m April. The website is usually where it starts.

Give me a website to look at and I’ll probably ask about your search, socials and emails while I’m there. I like doing the bits that sit between them: figuring out what needs saying, writing it in your voice and making the photos, video or other creative that go with it.

I’m based in Melbourne’s northern suburbs, and I work remotely. A lot of my favourite work has been with holistic practices, spas and tea brands. I’ve also spent more time thinking about dock levellers than I ever expected.

I’m a Gemini, which is a tidy way of saying I’m left-brained and right-brained at the same time. SEO and the build on one side, the look and the words on the other, often in the same afternoon.

Where I’ve worked

Sunflower Community Supports · Marketing & Operations Manager
I’ve been here since October 2025. The public-facing side of the role includes the website rebuild, Google and Facebook ads, socials and industry expos.

Capstone Industries · Social Media Manager, SEO Specialist and Web Designer
I worked part-time across 2023 and 2024. I designed and built the company website, worked on search and social, and redid sales material from brochures to spec sheets.

Tomedia · Project Manager and Content Writer
I worked on websites, marketing and content across several of the projects you’ll see here. Those are credited to Tomedia on the relevant pages.

What I’d like to work on next

I’m open to remote freelance projects. I’d love to hear from more wellness brands. I’m also keen to work with good people in other industries, especially if you’ve got a few bits of marketing that need to start working together.

And if the week is being eaten by admin, I can set up a simple AI workflow and show you how to run it, so it doesn’t sit there unused.
