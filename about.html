About

A marketer for brands that want the whole picture handled.

April Wray works from Melbourne’s northern suburbs with clients who can be anywhere. The portfolio leads with holistic and wellness brands, and it includes the other industries she has already done the work for.

She builds and looks after websites, search, social, copy, ads and email, and she makes the creative that sits around a brand: product photography, video, guides, calendars, cookbooks, tarot images and campaign artwork.

Where the work has been

Right now she is Marketing & Operations Manager at Sunflower Community Supports, a registered NDIS provider, from October 2025. That page stays general. It is a public website and a marketing brief, not a record of participants.

Before that she was Social Media Manager, SEO Specialist and Web Designer on a part-time contract at Capstone Industries in Campbellfield, Victoria, through 2023 and 2024.

She was also Project Manager and Content Writer at Tomedia. Several case studies here were delivered in that role. Where a live footer still says “Site by Tomedia” or “Made by Tomedia”, that credit stays on the page.

What she is looking for

Remote freelance clients. A wellness brand is the natural fit. A fashion label, a builder, a manufacturer or a community product is welcome too, when the work is the same craft: a clear site, steady marketing, and creative that belongs to the brand.
